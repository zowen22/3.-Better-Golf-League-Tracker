Design review · mobile navigation

Six ways to wayfind.

Right now the site has roughly 40 destinations sitting behind five groups — League, Stats & Records, Community, Admin, and Account — plus the season switcher. On desktop that's a hamburger drawer with no size limit. On a phone, that same list is the whole problem: how much do you show at rest, and how many taps to the thing someone actually wants?

Below are seven structurally different answers, each built as a working mockup of the real app — same green & terracotta, same content, same items. The front-runners lead: Scorecard Tabs (★, shipped as the overall nav shell), Large Nested Hamburger (★, modeled on GLT, its Stats branch originally just a comparison reference), and Flat Stats With In-Page Nav (✓ shipped 2026-08-02) — the real answer to that reference once @user asked for the Stats branch itself to actually mirror GLT's flat, categorized statistics directory instead of staying a nested accordion. Tap around inside each phone. The comparison table at the end lines them up on the same four questions.

Front-runner #1 · already shipped

Scorecard Tabs

A sticky strip of group tabs under the header. League, Community, and Admin unfold a second row of links right there. Stats & Records is the one exception — tap it and you're taken straight to a dedicated Stats home instead, picked up by a header badge and its own three-tier filter: category, a dependent sub-category scroller, then a time range, all before any content loads.

Why Stats gets its own page

  • 11 categories won't fit a scrollable tab row without hunting
  • A full-width dropdown shows every option in one tap

Watch for

  • One tab now behaves differently from the other three
  • Header badge is the only cue you've left the normal shell
4 tap targets at rest 1–2 taps to any item Reach: upper thumb zone

Front-runner #2 · modeled on a real competitor

Large Nested Hamburger

Opens down under the header, not over it — one full-screen drawer that fills the space below. League, Community, Admin, and My Account are flat single-tap-to-expand lists of the site's own real items. Stats is the one branch with real depth, expanding through Golf League Tracker's actual statistics-page structure — fetched live from a logged-in Buckeye League session (/glthome/statistics/): six categories, ~28 reports, two of them nesting a further nine sub-groups.

Best for

  • Leagues migrating from GLT who already know this shape
  • Power users who want the complete report list, not a curated subset

Watch for

  • Stats goes four levels deep in places (Stats → category → sub-group → report)
  • Most GLT report names here don't have a BGLT equivalent yet
  • Superseded 2026-08-02 — see Flat Stats With In-Page Nav, the shipped, flattened take on this same branch
28 reports, fully enumerated 2–4 taps depending on depth Reach: full-screen, under the header

Flat Stats With In-Page Nav

Same nested hamburger shell as F, but Stats & Records is now a single direct leaf, not a four-deep accordion. Tap it once and land on a flat, categorized directory — Individual, League, Standings, Contests, Hall of Fame — every real report visible on one scroll, mirroring GLT's own /glthome/statistics/ layout. Tap any report and its page carries a horizontal scrollable category tab bar up top (the same pattern already shipped on Standings), so you can hop to a sibling report without ever going back to the directory.

Best for

  • Everyone — replaces the old two-subgroup dropdown outright
  • Comparing several reports in the same category back-to-back

Watch for

  • Standings/Contests/Hall of Fame keep their own existing top-level entry — the directory just links out to them, not a full re-list
  • Category subnav ships as a shared Jinja include (one partial per category), not yet extended to Standings/Contests/Hall of Fame's own existing subnavs
19 reports, all real & live 2 taps to any report, flat Reach: full-screen directory, in-page pills on leaf

Mini Dock + Accordion

A slim, icon-only strip for the three most reflexive taps, plus a menu toggle that expands an ordinary accordion in the page itself — no overlay, no sheet, just scrolling further.

Best for

  • Users who find overlays disorienting
  • Keeping muscle memory close to a normal web page

Watch for

  • Pushes real content down while open
  • Icon-only strip needs very legible glyphs

For people who already know

Command Palette

Three icon shortcuts for the reflexive taps; everything else lives behind one search field that filters all 40 destinations as you type — built for admins who know exactly what they want and don't want to browse for it.

Best for

  • League admins running score entry mid-round
  • Power users who'd rather type than tap-tap-tap

Watch for

  • Needs exact-enough labels to be findable by typing
  • Least discoverable for a brand-new visitor
3 icons + search at rest 2 taps + typing to any item Reach: top, full-screen search
