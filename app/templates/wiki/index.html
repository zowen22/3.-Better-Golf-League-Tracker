Default Tees & Tee Selection Order

Every active player can have a personal Default Tee (set one at a time on their player profile, or in bulk from Admin → Default Tees) — the tee color that pre-fills for them at score entry instead of leaving everyone on the course's own default. This is separate from scoring on its own: setting a default tee doesn't change anyone's handicap or points by itself. It only decides which tee's yardage/par/handicap-index data gets pre-selected — but whichever tee is actually used for a scorecard does drive that round's net score and points, since par and handicap allocation are tee-specific.

A player's tee is worked out in this order, lowest to highest priority:

  1. The course's own default tee (set once per course).
  2. The player's personal Default Tee, if one is set — overrides the course default.
  3. A one-week tee change made from the print-scorecards popover — overrides both, for that single matchup only.

Picking a tee from the print-scorecards popover before a round also updates that player's personal Default Tee automatically — a tee picked once is what's remembered going forward, with no separate "save as default" step. Two exceptions: a hybrid pick (two different tees combined on one card) only ever affects that week's card, since a personal default can only hold one tee; and if a substitute is playing in someone's place, the sub's tee pick doesn't change the absent player's own personal default.
